Internet Journal
Academic Publishing as Practice

Internet Journal is an editorial platform founded inside the Digital Laboratory at Cal Poly Humboldt — built in partnership with the university's Digital Commons to publish student and faculty work outside the usual gatekeeping of academic publishing. The project treats editorial design as a discipline of attention: what gets named, what gets placed next to what, and how a body of work accumulates meaning across issues. Indexed on Google Scholar; ongoing as Editor-in-Chief.
